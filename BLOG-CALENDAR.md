## Content Repurposing Plan

Each blog article should be repurposed into:

1. **LinkedIn post** — Key takeaway + link (see LinkedIn generator)
2. **Instagram carousel** — 5–7 slide visual summary (see IG generator)
3. **Twitter/X thread** — 5–10 tweet thread of highlights
4. **Newsletter segment** — Summary + link in bi-weekly newsletter
5. **Video script** (Phase 2) — 3–5 minute explainer based on article
