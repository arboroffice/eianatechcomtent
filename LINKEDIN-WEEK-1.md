## WEDNESDAY — Hook: List / Framework
**Pillar:** Technical Guide (made accessible)
**Topic:** Choosing the right tech stack
**Best time to post:** 8:00–10:00 AM

---

Stop choosing tech stacks based on hype.

I've watched teams pick tools because:

↳ "It's what Google uses"
↳ "The CEO saw it at a conference"
↳ "A blog post said it's the future"

None of those are strategies.

Here's a 5-step framework we use at ElianaTech
to help teams make the right call:

1. Define the problem in one sentence
   → If you can't, you're not ready to choose anything

2. List your constraints
   → Budget. Timeline. Team skills. Scale requirements.

3. Evaluate 3 options max
   → More than 3 = analysis paralysis

4. Build a 2-week proof of concept
   → Not a 6-month pilot. Two weeks. Ship something.

5. Let the data decide
   → Not opinions. Not politics. Data.

Simple?

Yes.

That's the point.

The best tech decisions are boring ones
made with clear thinking.

Save this for next time you're evaluating tools. 📌

#TechStack #SoftwareDevelopment #CTO #ElianaTech #BuildSmart

### Engagement Strategy (per Lara's method)

**Before posting:**
- Spend 15 min commenting on 10 posts from your target audience
- Leave thoughtful, value-adding comments (not "Great post!")

**After posting:**
- Reply to EVERY comment in the first 2 hours
- Ask follow-up questions in your replies to keep threads going
- Like and reply to comments for the first 24 hours

**Daily habit:**
- 95% of growth comes from commenting, not posting
- Comment on 20-30 posts per day in your niche
- Be the most helpful person in every comment section
